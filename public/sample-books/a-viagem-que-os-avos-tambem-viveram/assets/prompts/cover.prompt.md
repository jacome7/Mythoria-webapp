# Cover prompt

Warm watercolor portrait book cover for the fictional Portuguese family story “A Viagem que os Avós Também Viveram”: a split but harmonious view of the same fictional village square in two eras, grandparents in an old generic photo and grandchildren in the present, no identifiable real people, soft paper texture, cream, teal and warm sepia. Cover art only, no mockup, no logo, no watermark. Render the title exactly once and legibly.
