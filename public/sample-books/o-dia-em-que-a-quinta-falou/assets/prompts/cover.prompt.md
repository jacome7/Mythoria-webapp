# Cover prompt

Warm hand-drawn portrait book cover for the fictional Portuguese children’s story “O Dia em que a Quinta Falou”: a fully clothed five-year-old girl safely beside her grandfather under an old cork oak, with a few calm farm animals behind a fence, visible pencil strokes, organic lines, warm cream, green and ochre. Cover art only, no mockup, no logo, no real farm, no watermark. Render the title exactly once and legibly.
